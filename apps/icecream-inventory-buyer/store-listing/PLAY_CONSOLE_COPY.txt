Full description
================================================================================
Jora Trade is an invite-only B2B ordering app for distributors and customers.

How access works
• Your ice cream manufacturer (or supplier) creates an invite in their BuildFlow inventory app.
• They share an invite code with you.
• You open Jora Trade, enter that code, and open their catalog.
• There is no public signup and no email/phone password login.
• Use the same invite code anytime to return to your manufacturer’s listings.

What you can do
• Browse the catalog your supplier publishes for you (role-based pricing when configured).
• Add quantities, review your cart, and place orders.
• See recent order history and update your account details.

Jora Trade is built for wholesale ordering across product lines — not limited to a single category.
