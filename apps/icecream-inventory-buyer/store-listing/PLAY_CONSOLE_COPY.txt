Full description

Jora Trade is an invite-only B2B ordering app for distributors and customers.

How access works
• Your ice cream manufacturer (or supplier) creates an invite for you in their BuildFlow inventory app.
• They share a one-time invite code with you.
• You open Jora Trade, enter that code, confirm your details, and join.
• There is no public signup. You cannot use the app without a code from your manufacturer.
• After you have joined once, you can sign in again with the email or mobile OTP registered on your invite.

What you can do
• Browse the catalog your supplier publishes for you (role-based pricing when configured).
• Add quantities, review your cart, and place orders.
• See recent order history and update your account details.

Jora Trade is built for wholesale ordering across product lines — not limited to a single category.
