Delete your account & data

For Jora Trade (buyer app) and BuildFlow products operated by StaffingPros / Jora AI. Last updated: 18 October 2026.

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated personal data. Requests are handled by StaffingPros / Jora AI support (or your manufacturer admin for buyer invites).

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to vssnreddy1@gmail.com (the developer contact for Jora Trade / BuildFlow).
  2. Use subject line: Account deletion request — Jora Trade / BuildFlow.
  3. Include: your full name, the email or phone on the account (if any), company / manufacturer name, and the invite code or buyer account identifier if you use Jora Trade.
  4. We will confirm the request and delete or anonymize your personal account data within 30 days (sooner when practical), subject to legal retention needs below.

Email deletion request

What is deleted

  • Buyer / user account profile (name, email, phone, addresses you provided).
  • Login / invite association so you can no longer access the app with that account.
  • Personal contact details stored on your buyer profile.

What may be kept (and for how long)

  • Business transaction records (for example sales orders, invoices, GST records) may be retained as required for tax, accounting, and legal compliance — typically up to the period required by applicable law (often several years). Personal identifiers on those records may be anonymized where possible.
  • Security / backup logs may be retained for a limited period (usually up to 90 days) for fraud prevention and disaster recovery, then purged on the normal backup cycle.
  • Data your manufacturer / company admin keeps in their own workspace (for example party master records they created) may need to be deleted by that admin as well; we can help coordinate when you write to us.

Jora Trade (invite-only buyers)

Jora Trade does not use a public signup. Access is by manufacturer invite code. Deleting your buyer account disables further access with that invite binding. You may also ask your manufacturer to revoke buyer access from their BuildFlow inventory app.
