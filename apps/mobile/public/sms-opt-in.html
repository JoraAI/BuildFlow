SMS opt-in (StaffingPros / Jora AI)

How users consent to authentication texts for the BuildFlow platform.

StaffingPros and Jora AI operate BuildFlow at https://build-flow-frontend-jet.vercel.app/. End users consent to receive transactional SMS one-time passcodes when they provide a mobile number and check the SMS consent box before requesting an OTP on login or invite acceptance.

Message types
